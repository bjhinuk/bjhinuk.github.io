Teaching

I have taught courses in introductory microeconomics, macroeconomics, and statistics at the University of Oklahoma.

Instructor of Record

ECON 1113 — Principles of Macroeconomics

Summer 2025

An introductory course covering fundamental principles of macroeconomics, including economic growth, unemployment, inflation, fiscal and monetary policy, and international trade. The course emphasizes applying economic concepts to understand current economic issues and policy questions.

ECON 1123 — Principles of Microeconomics

Summer 2026

An introductory course covering the basic principles of microeconomics, including supply and demand, consumer and producer behavior, market structures, market failures, and the role of government in the economy. The course focuses on using economic reasoning to analyze individual and market-level decisions.

ECON 1113 — Principles of Macroeconomics

Fall 2026

An introductory course covering fundamental principles of macroeconomics, including economic growth, unemployment, inflation, fiscal and monetary policy, and international trade. The course emphasizes understanding macroeconomic relationships and applying economic concepts to real-world economic issues.

Teaching Assistant

ECON 2843 — Elements of Statistics

Fall 2023, Spring 2024, Fall 2024, Spring 2025

An introductory statistics course covering descriptive statistics, probability, statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and regression analysis. As a teaching assistant, I supported students in understanding statistical concepts and applying quantitative methods to economic problems.
