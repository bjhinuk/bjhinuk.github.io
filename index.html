Jhinuk Banerjee

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Economics at the University of Oklahoma. My research interests lie at the intersection of Political Economy, Gender Economics, and Development Economics. My research studies how economic conditions and institutions shape political representation, political behavior, and public policy, with a particular interest in gender and political decision-making.
